The following disclosures are provided in accordance with British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), the Insurance Act (BC), and the Insurance Council of British Columbia Code of Conduct.
Collection, Use & Disclosure of Personal Information (PIPA). I/we consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of my/our personal information by AP Insurance Services Ltd. and its representatives for the purposes of assessing insurability, underwriting, rating, policy administration, and claims handling. I understand my information may be shared with insurers, reinsurers, underwriters, adjusters, and industry claims-history databases (e.g. Insurance Bureau of Canada) as reasonably required for these purposes, and that I may contact AP Insurance Services Ltd.'s Privacy Officer to review, correct, or ask questions about my personal information.
Credit Check Consent (Soft Credit Check). To collect only my personal credit information, including my credit score, from consumer reporting agencies, as permitted by law for the purposes identified above. I understand that my consent for the use of credit information remains valid until withdrawn in writing, and that withdrawing or failing to provide consent may mean I do not benefit from the best rate available to me.
Consent to Verify Insurance, Claims History & Background Information. I authorize AP Insurance Services Ltd. and its underwriters to obtain and exchange information about my prior insurance policies, claims and loss history, and other background information reasonably required to assess this application (including property, occupancy, and — where applicable — driving record information) with previous insurers, industry databases, and other relevant parties, for underwriting and rating purposes only.
Accuracy of Information. I/we declare that the information provided in this application is true, complete, and accurate to the best of my/our knowledge, and that it will form part of the basis of any contract of insurance issued. I understand that any misrepresentation, omission, or false statement may result in denial of coverage, cancellation of the policy, or denial of a claim, in accordance with the Insurance Act (British Columbia).
Fraud Warning. Insurance fraud is a criminal offence. Any person who knowingly provides false, incomplete, or misleading information on this application may be subject to denial of coverage, policy cancellation, and/or prosecution under the Criminal Code of Canada.
Application Is Not a Binding Contract. This form is a request for quotation only and does not bind coverage. Coverage takes effect only when confirmed in writing by the underwriting insurer and any required premium has been received. Final terms, conditions, premium, and coverage are subject to underwriting approval.
Broker / Agent Relationship & Compensation Disclosure. AP Insurance Services Ltd. is licensed with the Insurance Council of British Columbia and may act as agent for one or more insurers. AP Insurance Services Ltd. may receive commission or other compensation from the insurer(s) for policies placed on your behalf; further detail is available on request.
